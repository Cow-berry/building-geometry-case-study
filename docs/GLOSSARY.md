# Glossary of building temrminology
## General
Site Polygon: the initial polygon within which we are allowed to build
Inset: a polygon whose points are inside the site polygon, and at least a set distance from the site polygon
Footprint: the true polygon above which all of the building stands. Footprint must be within the Inset.

## Constraints
Setback: the low limit on the distance between the Site Polygon and the Inset
Maximum height: the high limit on the total height
Maximum floor count: the high limit on the floor count
Floor-to-floor height: the constant distance between two adjacent floors (implicit assumption that all floors are of equal height)
Site Coverage Ratio: Footprint area / Site Polygon area

## Targets
Gross Footprint Area: Foorprint area * floor count
Floor Area Ratio: Gross Foorprint area / Site Polygon area
